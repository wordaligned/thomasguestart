This was carved from softcut using Essdee tools. The eel is carved positive and negative, clockwise and anticlockwise. Hand printed using a baren, Cranfield Caligo ink on seawhite cartridge paper.

It was inspired by a small conger eel found in the rockpools at Oxwich Bay on a low tide.
